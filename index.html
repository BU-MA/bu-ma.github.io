Theorem of the Week (#5)

Theorem (Yoneda's Lemma). Let \( \mathcal{C} \) be a locally small category, let \( \mathcal{S}et \) be the category of sets, and let \( F : \mathcal{C}\to\mathcal{S}et \) be any functor. Then for all objects \( x\in\mathcal{C}\), there is an isomorphism of sets (i.e. a bijection) \[ \varphi_x : \operatorname{Nat}(\mathcal{C}(x,-),F)\cong F(x), \] natural in \( x \).

What is BUMA?

We're a community of people who are enthusiastic about all mathematics, and related fields like physics and CS. We host students from a wide range of backgrounds, from freshmen who haven't taken calculus, to seniors who are writing theses on topics that sound completely made up.
