About BUMA

\(e^{i\pi}=-1\)

The Boston University Mathematics Association brings together the mathematics department for talks, problem-solving sessions, social events, and academic advising.

Our goal is to make mathematics accessible to everyone, and to foster the mathematical community here at BU. Membership is extremely low-committment, and the only prerequisite is interest.

What We Do

Putnam Problem Solving

Fall semester sessions led by Professor David Fried. We solve practice problems for the annual Putnam exam, a difficult and prestigious competitive math exam for college students. This is the closest BU has to a proper competitive math team. Feel free to come and tryhard the practice, or just hang out and have fun solving some tricky problems.

Talks

Professor-led and student-led talks on anything we consider interesting. Our topics range from music theory to the most abstract nonsense humanity has conceived of. Prerequisites are kept minimal.

Social Events

Puzzle nights, ice cream socials, trivial nights, competitions, and more. Often in collaboration with other clubs.

Integration Bee

A semesterly event inspired by MIT's, but less competitive and more fun. Come to play, hang out, or just watch physicists struggle with Gaussians.

Advising

Semesterly sessions on course registration led by upperclassmen. Additional occasional sessions on research, REUs, and graduate school applications.

For exact dates and times, see the events page. Join the Discord to stay up to date.

Our meetings usually start about 5-10 minutes after the scheduled time to allow for people to arrive late, and to give some time to chat. The meeting itself can either wrap up 10 minutes early, or run 20 minutes over, or just devolve back into aimless chatting and socializing, depending on how everyone is feeling.

There's no requirement for you to stay for the full meeting, or for you to come to every meeting. If you think one of our meetings looks interesting or fun, feel free to swing by.

We try to bring pizza when we can, but BU club guidelines have made this difficult recently, so we can't guarentee it unfortunately. However, we can guarentee food for bigger events, and (small) prizes for competitions.
