Fall 2026 Newsletter 2 E-Board Recruitment

Grant Talbert · September 21, 2026 · 8 min read

Fall 2026

We're recruiting for our E-Board! We're looking to bring on 3-4 people who are enthusiastic about fostering the BU mathematics community to join us in running the club. This newsletter is all about the details, so you can see if this is something you're interested in.

Our Current E-Board

Our current board has 5 members. 4 of them are seniors, and the other one is also leaving after this year. Additionally, since most of us are really busy, we don't have enough time to get all the things done for the club that we want. Needless to say, we really need to recruit new members for our board to help us out, and to keep the club running once we're gone.

However, we don't need to recruit a whole new board. We're working on a merger with the Society of Mathematics (SOM), a sister club of ours, and we'll probably transfer the positions of president, vice president, treasurer, and secretary to their current board. As such, we're working with the SOM board during this recruitment process, and we both agree that we need more manpower on the board to accomplish the things we want.

What We're Looking For

We want to recruit 3-4 new members for the board. Past experience on an E-Board is a benefit, but absolutely not a requirement, and we're committed to training anyone who needs training. We're not necessarily looking for people who are amazing at math, because more than anything else, we want to find people who are enthusiastic and willing to get involved.

We're also particularly interested in getting a wider variety of people on the board. Historically, BUMA's board has been pretty heavily rooted in pure math, and as a result we haven't been supporting the whole community, just the pure math part. We want to change that, and are actively encouraging students interested in applied math, statistics, or other math-adjacent topics (physics, cs, econ, etc.) to apply. We still of course want pure math students to apply.

You don't need to come to every single event we host (some of our current board doesn't lol). If you have ideas about things you wish the math community at BU did more of, then that's enough to consider applying. A lot of what we want from the next board is people who will bring in perspectives and ideas that the current board would not necessarily come up with on its own.

What Happens if You're Selected

For the rest of this semester, being on the board will be kind of like a training period. The idea is that you'll spend this semester getting used to how we run things and gradually taking on more responsibility as we show you how things work here. For example, you'll be asked to help prepare for events, and we have 2 events reserved later on in the semester for the new board members to brainstorm and run by themselves. You might also be asked to help out with other stuff, like creating graphics, sending emails, contacting another club, or writing one of these newsletters.

In addition, we will show you how to submit food orders with the math department, how to (not) deal with the administrative nightmare that BU has created with SLIC and DOSBO and AB, how to update this website, how to request a venue for an event, how to submit a funding request to AB, and probably more. We don't expect everyone to be good at everything, but we want everyone to know how stuff works around here, so if you need to help out somewhere, you can. We're also trying to gauge where you will be best able to help out next semester. So if you're good at planning for events, you might have a big role in helping us with brainstorming. If you know web design, then I would love to stop being the only person touching this website omfg please i dont have time for this please please plaeases

Next semester, everyone will start getting more important jobs. Since most of the current board is graduating, we'll be spending the spring transferring more and more of the actual management of the club to the next board. The goal is basically that by the time we leave, the new board already knows how everything works and has actually had experience running it.

We're all students and we know everyone gets busy, so we're not expecting BUMA to become your entire life. That's part of why we're even recruiting: most of our board members just don't have time to do everything. With that said, being on the board does require some amount of consistency. If you agree to handle something for an event, we need to be able to trust that it will get done, or that you'll tell us if you need help.

The amount of work varies a lot from week to week. Some weeks there's not a ton we have to do, but around big events and around the start of the semester, there's a ton of work to do. We care much more about people being communicative and reliable than about everyone having an enormous amount of free time, that way we can properly delegate work in advance to ensure it gets done.

Applications and Interviews

Applications will be open for two weeks. After that, we'll go through the responses with the SOM board and invite some applicants to short interviews, probably during the following week. These will not be anything insanely formal; we mostly just want to talk to people a little more, hear what you're interested in, and get a better idea of what you might want to do on the board.

Also, please don't decide not to apply because you think you aren't qualified enough. If you're interested but don't have much experience, that's fine. Again, this semester is specifically supposed to give new people time to learn how all of this works. We'd much rather get an application from someone who is genuinely excited about the club than from someone who has a perfect resume and doesn't really care.

Club Updates

Food and Funding

As those who came to our Putnam session last Monday already know, we have obtained clearance to have food for our meetings. However, this is contingent on us not making a mess. If we leave the room messy, we will no longer be able to have food for any meetings. Thus, we are asking you all to be mindful when you eat. Try not to make a mess. If you get food on the table or floor, clean it up. Your support is what makes these meetings possible.

We also have some specifics we can share about our club budget. The math department has provided us with $300 for the year to spend as we please. However, if we use this money for food, the math department would like us to use their catering platform, which is unfortunately more expensive than simply walking to Domino's and buying some pizza. As such, we're not sure how many events we can fund with this money. We think this will be good for 6 events, and will try our best to increase that number. But for now, we will be saving this money for our most important events.

In addition to the above $300, we have been told that the department has provided us with funds specifically for the Putnam sessions. We are still unsure how to use this money, but we expect to figure this out by next week. Last week, our E-Board member Bala Muralitharan generously donated a number of his dining points for us to spend on pizza for the Putnam session. Thanks, Bala!

We have also gotten our semesterly funding from the Allocations Board (AB). The way these funds work is we submit a request for funding for up to 3 events, and AB funds these events as they see fit. They have chosen to fund 2 of our 3 requested events this year. In past years, we would be able to use the funds they gave us however we wanted, even if it wasn't how we initially intended to spend them. However, AB is being much more strict this year, and we are required to spend these funds for the precise events that we requested them for. Additionally, we must spend these funds this year, or the funds will be removed from our account. Thus, you can expect the following two events sometime this year.

  • Math-Interdisciplinary Mixer: A mixer for math and math-adjacent majors of all types, especially STEM majors. We're hoping to encourage attendance from other schools in the Boston area. This event will likely be hosted in the Spring, as it will take time to plan.
  • Ice Cream Social: The ice cream social is exactly what it sounds like; we buy some ice cream, and people come to eat and hang out. This is an event we held at the start of both semesters last year, but didn't have the funding to host at the beginning of this year. We're unsure when we will host it, but it will likely be sometime soon, possibly in addition to our usual event lineup. We may ask about the best time to schedule this at a meeting.

Wrapping Up

This is a pretty important transition year for BUMA. The current board has gotten to build a lot over the past couple of years, but if the club is going to keep growing, it needs a new group of people who actually care about where it goes next.

As always, you can also talk to any of us at an event or message us on Discord if you have questions. Thanks for reading, and we'll hopefully see your application soon!
