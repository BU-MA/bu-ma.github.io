Monday 21 5:30 PM  ·  CDS 365
Putnam Problem Solving Session
We solve problems from previous Putnam exams, and other difficult, Putnam-style problems. These problems should be accessible to most undergraduate students with an understanding of calculus and proofs. Hosted in collaboration with Professor David Fried.
 
Wednesday 23 5:00 PM  ·  CDS 548
Introduction to Differential Geometry
Differential geometry starts with the question of "how do I do calculus on a surface?" The answer to this question is a fascinating blend of calculus, topology, algebra, and more. Differential geometry also sits at the heart of modern mathematical physics, making it an extremely applicable field of math. In this talk we will introduce differential geometry, and discuss a couple of its applications to math and physics. Calculus background will be assumed, and linear algebra background will be helpful.
