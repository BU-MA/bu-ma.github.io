Hi all! We hope everyone had fun last week at our first meeting. We have two events this week, including the return of our Putnam practice sessions this Monday (today).
This week’s meetings
Monday 14 5:30 PM  ·  CDS 365
Putnam Problem Solving Session
We solve problems from previous Putnam exams, and other difficult, Putnam-style problems. These problems should be accessible to most undergraduate students with an understanding of calculus and proofs. Hosted in collaboration with Professor David Fried.
 
Wednesday 16 5:00 PM  ·  CDS 548
Celebrating this year's Fields medalists
The Fields Medal is an annual award for amazing young mathematicians, and is frequently called the "Nobel Prize of Mathematics." We'll be honoring this year's Fields Medalists, as well as honoring the mathematician Jean-Pierre Serre's 100th birthday, which is on Tuesday, September 15th!
Notes & announcements
Meeting Locations. We have confirmed with the department that all our Monday Putnam sessions will be in CDS 365, and all our Wednesday general meetings will be in CDS 548.
Food. Starting next week, we will have pizza at all Putnam practice sessions, budget permitting. We will notify everyone if/when it becomes no longer financially feasible to order food.
