Grant Talbert

vice president

I'm a senior, but I've only been at BU for 3 years since I transferred. I love discussing and writing about advanced and abstract math, and spend a lot of my free time reading or writing about interesting mathematical topics.

Although I am officially the vice president, internally we think of Katherine and me as "copresidents", and we take on similar responsibilities. I handle planning for a lot of our events, as well as the website (along with some help from Bala), E-board recruitment, and some collaborations with other clubs. I am also somewhat involved in our financials and social media presence. Feel free to reach out to me directly with any questions or requests about the club, or contact the club directly.

My main interest is in mathematically rigorous quantum field theory. I'm currently learning about the factorization algebras of Kevin Costello and Owen Gwilliam, and the BV-formalism. I'm also interested in topological QFT, homotopy theory, and \(\infty\)-category theory. This means I work heavily with differential and algebraic geometry, homological algebra, and related fields Outside of math, I like milk tea, ice skating, food, and playing Minecraft.

I’m a rising senior at BU and the treasurer of BUMA, majoring in (Pure) Mathematics and minoring in Computer Science.

My research interests are in number theory, including both algebraic number theory and analytic number theory, with a focus on Elliptic curves and Modular forms. Recently, I’ve been learning about murmuration theory and the BSD-conjecture. Additionally, I’m learning some basic coding in Magma and Sage to gain experience with computational number theory. Beyond my primary research interests, I'm also interested in knot theory, and the use of AI in math.

If you are interested in our club, or any of the topics listed above, feel free to contact me via email.
